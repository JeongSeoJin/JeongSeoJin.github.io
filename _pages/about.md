Hello! I am a first-year Mechanical Engineering student in South Korea and a robotics enthusiast who loves building robots from scratch.

I am eager to study mathematics, physics and robotics to deeply understand my robots and apply theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges through hands-on projects. I'm interested in building robots that can assist people and improve our daily lives. To achieve this, I'm currently focusing on building a 'Cycloidal QDD Actuator' and planning to integrate it into a complex robotic systems such as bipedal robots, bi-manipulators and humanoids.

I strongly believe in the power of open-source. Growing up, I was deeply inspired by roboticists who shared their resources and informative videos. Now, it's my turn to give back. I want to contribute to and inspire the robotics community—helping researchers, students, and hobbyists just I've been inspired to others. That is why I share my all projects as open-source resources.

I document my journey and resources on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/engineering.seojin_n.n?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet&igsh=ZDNlZDc0MzIxNw==), [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@engineeringseojin?si=F3yA-zbrFuG3kMWH), and [GitHub](https://github.com/JeongSeoJin) to share inspiration and engage in critical discussions. I'm constantly learning and building, so feel free to reach out to me and follow my upcoming projects!
