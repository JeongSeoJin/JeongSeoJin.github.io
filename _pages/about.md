Hello! I am a first-year Mechanical Engineering student in Seoul, Korea and a robotics enthusiast who loves building robots from scratch.

My ultimate goal is to become a 'Full-Stack Robotics Engineer & Scientist'. Currently, I am deeply into Actuator Design & Control. I enjoy the entire process of robotics, from 3D Mechanical Design and CAE to Control Theory, Machine Learning, Mathematics, and Physics.

I believe in the power of open-source. I want to contribute to the robotics community—helping researchers, students, and hobbyists alike. That is why I share my projects as open-source resources.

I document my journey and resources on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/engineering.seojin_n.n?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet&igsh=ZDNlZDc0MzIxNw==), [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@engineeringseojin?si=F3yA-zbrFuG3kMWH), and [GitHub](https://github.com/JeongSeoJin) to share inspiration and engage in critical discussions. I am growing every day, so please look forward to my future projects!
